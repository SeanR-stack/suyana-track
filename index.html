Where to look first

What this is, and what it is not

Real ground, real readings. Every parcel is a public OpenStreetMap farmland boundary in Brazil, Colombia or Argentina. Weather is measured: 60-day rainfall and soil moisture from the Open-Meteo archive through Nov 8, compared against the same source's own 2021-2025 history for the same dates. Satellite readings are per-pixel vegetation (NDVI) measured inside the parcel boundary from Sentinel-2 scenes at 10 m resolution.

Fictional loans. The portfolio, loan IDs and amounts are invented for this demo. These are real farms on a map, not Suyana customers.

Flags say where to look, not who will default. A low vegetation reading can follow harvest, planting or crop stage as easily as stress. A rain deficit is a reason to call the farmer, not proof of loss. Nothing here predicts default.

Ranking. Sorted by the size of each parcel's rainfall anomaly against its own 5-year baseline. Soil moisture is shown as context only (raw volumetric value, 0-7 cm depth), not judged against a seasonal target. The point is investigation order, not a score.

Stale data is shown, not hidden. Every satellite reading carries its scene date. Cloudy stretches mean some readings are weeks old, and that age is printed next to the number.
